Airport Indicator v5

Level rules (draft)
LevelWhenWhat it means for a traveler
NormalThe FAA has published no restriction for the airport.Operating as planned.
LimitedSmall delay (reported maximum of 30 minutes or less); low-visibility or instrument-approach runway configuration; announced upcoming closure (NOTAM); inside a daily closure window.A few minutes of slippage is possible; most flights are barely affected.
DelayGround Delay Program with average delay under 60 minutes, or a general delay with a maximum above 30 minutes.Affected flights are held on the ground; watch your flight status.
SevereGround Stop; Ground Delay Program with average delay of 60 minutes or more; airport closed.Affected flights are likely to be held for a long time or stopped.
The FAA reports general delays in steps (16-30, 31-45 minutes ...), so there is no "under 15 minutes" value; "30 minutes or less" is the lowest step. De-icing notices are shown as information only and do not change the level (they stay active from the start until the next morning). Operations Plan outlooks ("possible", "expected") are not part of the level yet. The Trend field is not used because it proved unreliable.

Ground Stop / GDP event log

When the FAA extends or converts an event it issues a new id; here they are merged by airport + start time (versions = number of distinct ids seen). Each advisory was fetched from the link carried by the event, then read with fixed rules (no AI), so the lines under it are the advisory's own fields.
Data: FAA NAS Status JSON snapshots ( change snapshots) plus the ATCSCC advisories those events link to. Built locally; no server was touched. The FAA feed lists only airports that currently have something to report; an airport not listed is shown as Normal.
